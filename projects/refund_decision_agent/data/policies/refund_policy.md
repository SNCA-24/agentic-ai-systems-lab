# Refund Policy

## Approved Refund Cases

- A confirmed duplicate charge is refundable when billing evidence shows the customer was charged twice for the same service period or invoice.
- A charge posted after a confirmed cancellation may be refunded when the cancellation timestamp predates the finalized charge.
- A refund request inside the refund window may be approved when the account evidence is complete and no other review rule applies.

## Refund Window

- The standard refund window is 30 days from the finalized charge date.
- Requests submitted after the 30-day refund window are normally ineligible unless another policy section explicitly overrides that outcome.

## Review Thresholds

- Any high-value refund request at or above $100.00 requires human review before a refund can be approved.
- Any enterprise refund request requires human review, even when the customer appears eligible under another rule.

## Escalation Rules

- If the available evidence is unclear, incomplete, or internally inconsistent, the request must be escalated instead of auto-decided.
- Conflicting evidence between policy retrieval, customer records, or billing records must be treated as an escalation case.
