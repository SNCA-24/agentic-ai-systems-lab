# Billing Policy

## Recurring Subscription Billing

- Standard recurring subscription charges are billed on the invoice date for the active billing period.
- Annual subscriptions may create a single annual invoice that covers the full term.

## Duplicate Charge Detection

- A duplicate charge exists when two finalized charges point to the same invoice or service period without a valid prorated explanation.
- Duplicate charge findings should be recorded using invoice identifiers, charge timestamps, and matching amounts.

## Review and Escalation Boundaries

- Enterprise billing cases require human review before any refund recommendation is finalized.
- Any high-value annual billing dispute at or above $100.00 requires human review.
- If invoice timing, duplicate evidence, or account history is conflicting evidence, the request must escalate rather than auto-decide.
